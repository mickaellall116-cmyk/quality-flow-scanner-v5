# HYPOTHESIS.md — ADX ranking under the canonical 12-step protocol

**Status: FROZEN before running. Written 2026-09-25, before any result in this
study is computed. No changes to the rule after seeing results.**

## 1. Hypothesis
When portfolio slots are scarce (a bar produces more valid signals than free
slots), ranking the contenders by ADX(14) — highest trend strength first —
selects better future trades than (a) arrival order (take-all, the do-nothing
counterfactual) and (b) the adopted rs_top2 rule (20-bar return minus SPY).
The prior confirmatory study (pine_ranking_confirm, 2026-09-25) found ADX
+0.314R vs take-all +0.221R vs rs_top2 +0.160R, with ADX beating the adopted
rule on every cut but beating take-all only in 2025 (21 genuine contests).
This study puts that claim through the full canonical protocol.

## 2. Mechanism
A breakout bets that demand overwhelms supply. When slots are limited, prefer
the setup whose trend is already most established (highest ADX). On this
concentrated momentum watchlist, highest 20-bar return tends to be the most
extended/latest name; trend strength at entry continues more reliably.

## 3. Exact rule (fixed, no tuning)
- Signal population: the identical canonical 4H Hybrid trades on the 14-stock
  watchlist (QQQ, SMCI, PLTR, ANET, SOFI, RKLB, ONDS, DRAM, SPCX, ASTX, BBAI,
  NIO, HOOD, AMD), Oct 2023–Sep 2026, frozen Mode B exits, $10k / 1% risk /
  max 5 concurrent / 5% heat, next-bar-open fills. Generated by
  `pb.gen_pine_trades` exactly as in pine_ranking_confirm.
- Features point-in-time at the signal bar: ADX(14) via `pb.adx(df, 14)`
  (same definition as P2/confirmatory). Perturbations: ADX(10), ADX(20).
- Ranking engages ONLY at contested timestamps: candidates > free slots.
  Score: ADX descending; take top min(2, free). (Identical engagement logic
  to pine_ranking_confirm.)
- Baselines on the SAME signal population: `takeall` (arrival order, cap 2)
  and `rs_spy_CONTROL` (the adopted rule, 20-bar return minus SPY).
- The RS+ADX combo is NOT tested (mandate decision: rs_spy does not beat
  take-all, so it is not evaluated).

## 4. Primary analysis unit (Mike's structure)
**Per-contest advantage on crowded bars.** At each contested timestamp with a
real choice (free slots > 0), ADX would take the top min(2, free) candidates
by ADX; take-all takes the first min(2, free) by arrival order. The contest
advantage = mean(net_r of ADX picks) − mean(net_r of take-all picks), using
net R at 25bps. This isolates the ranking decision from portfolio path
dependence: same bar, same candidates, same costs, different selection.
Secondary: full portfolio-sim differential (taken sets, expectancy, PF, DD)
for economic effect.

## 5. Date range / dev-validation / walk-forward
- Full sample: 2023-10-25 → 2026-09-23 (same as confirmatory).
- Dev / validation: DEV = signal_time ≤ 2024-12-31; VALIDATION =
  signal_time ≥ 2025-01-01. HONEST CAVEAT: the ADX rule itself (period 14,
  descending top-2) was defined in P2/confirmatory on the FULL sample, so
  this split is a period-robustness check, not a clean pre-registration.
  No parameter is re-fit on dev.
- Rolling walk-forward on the per-contest advantage (no parameters fit, so
  train windows are context only; test windows are the untouched evaluation):
  - W1: train 2023-10→2024-09, test 2024-10→2025-03
  - W2: train 2024-04→2025-03, test 2025-04→2025-09
  - W3: train 2024-10→2025-09, test 2025-10→2026-03
  - W4: train 2025-04→2026-03, test 2026-04→2026-09
  Combine the untouched test windows at the end.

## 6. Costs
25 / 50 / 75 / 100 bps on the differential. If the edge disappears with
modest friction, it is weak.

## 7. Perturbation (not optimization)
ADX(10), ADX(14), ADX(20). We do not care which is best; we care whether the
general idea (prefer stronger trend on crowded bars) survives nearby periods.

## 8. Concentration
Best 1, 3, 5 trades / symbols / sectors / years driving the ADX-vs-takeall
differential. Explicitly: how much of the differential comes from 2025
crowded bars?

## 9. Bootstrap
Resample the genuine per-contest advantages with replacement, 5000 draws,
mean-advantage distribution. Report the 5th/25th/50th/75th/95th percentiles
and the fraction of resample means > 0. Question: is positive advantage
typical, or is the observed result a lucky tail?

## 10. Leave-one-symbol-out
Recompute the per-contest mean advantage and the full-sim Δ expectancy
removing one symbol at a time. A robust edge should not collapse on any
single name.

## 11. Economic effect
Δ expectancy, Δ PF, Δ drawdown, trades selected/rejected (with expectancy of
ranked-out vs swapped-in trades), opportunity cost, return per unit of risk
(total return / max DD) — ADX vs take-all AND ADX vs rs_top2.

## 12. Forward
The ADX shadow on the live scoreboard is the forward arbiter for the
take-all question. Do not change it, do not touch production.

## Verdict gates (pre-declared)
Two separate verdicts, because replacing a harmful rule with a better one is
a different decision than adding ranking where none binds:

**Verdict A — ADX vs the adopted rs_top2 rule.**
- PASS: ADX beats rs_top2 on pooled expectancy, in dev AND validation, in a
  majority of years, at all cost levels, at nearby ADX periods, with
  two-sided selection skill (ranked-out expectancy < swapped-in expectancy),
  and without a single-symbol dependency.
- MAYBE: beats the rule pooled and on most cuts but thin differential
  sample leaves residual doubt.
- FAIL: does not beat the rule consistently.

**Verdict B — ADX vs take-all (do nothing).**
- PASS: per-contest advantage positive in dev AND validation, positive in
  the combined walk-forward test windows, positive in at least 3 of 4 years,
  survives 50–100bps, survives ADX(10)/ADX(20) perturbation, bootstrap median
  > 0 with ≥75% of resample means above 0, no single symbol/year supplies
  >50% of the differential.
- MAYBE: pooled advantage positive with two-sided skill and perturbation
  robustness, but concentrated in 2025 or thin enough that the differential
  sample alone cannot carry PASS.
- FAIL: advantage disappears or flips on validation / walk-forward test
  windows, or is a 2025-only phenomenon with neutral/negative elsewhere.
