# Research Framework Audit — Quality Flow Scanner (2026-09-24/25)

**Scope:** methodology audit only. No strategy optimization was performed. Nothing frozen was modified — no changes to V5.4 production, Mode B, alerts, grades, market gate, or the AI Observer. All worker scripts live under `research_audit/<area>/` and import `pine_backtest.py` unmodified.

**Question asked:** could any historical edge estimates be overstated because of data, universe, execution, or testing methodology?

**Short answer:** yes — materially. The +0.2388R baseline is overstated roughly 2× by universe selection alone; the research backtests implement zero Mode B exit features so they are not a valid prior for the forward test; a genuine look-ahead leak shrinks the lone-wolf edge ~14% and invalidates its "dead money" claim; and nothing survives a multiple-testing correction. The candidate *deltas* are less damaged than the baseline *level*, but every gate calibrated to +0.239R is miscalibrated.

## Adjusted confidence

**A. The +0.239R baseline — LOW.** Stack the findings: universe selection roughly halves it (selection-neutral ≈ +0.05–0.12R), portfolio realism takes another 9% off per taken trade with 23% of signals never taken, realistic costs shave 0.01–0.08R, and — most fundamentally — the research backtests implement *zero* Mode B exit features, so +0.239R is not a valid prior for the frozen forward-test system at all. The honest statement: a selection-neutral, deployable baseline is on the order of **+0.05–0.12R per signal before Mode B effects, which are unmeasured**. The forward test is the first real measurement of the frozen system.

**B. Lone-wolf sector RS — MEDIUM** (downgraded from the handover's PASS-with-caveats; strongest of a weak field). For: directionally consistent across validation (+0.146), walk-forward 4/4, all costs, all LOSO, strict-PIT survival (+0.0925R), placebo 95.6th percentile. Against: the published measurement leaked (signal-date close; −14% under PIT; "dead money" claim invalid), multiplicity-adjusted p = 0.84, universe level inflation, QQQ = 43% of the improvement. What moves it up: the live overlay blocking trades at the checkpoint. What moves it down: live blocks running at the historical blocked-trade rate without the retained outperformance.

**C. FVG-as-entry — LOW** (unchanged: FAIL). Failed the protocol, failed the placebo (67th percentile, p=0.33), failed multiplicity. Only new live sidecar data can reopen it.

**D. ADX ranking — LOW.** Vs take-all it is a ~1.5σ MAYBE (placebo 93rd percentile, p=0.07; 2025-concentrated; 21 contests; multiplicity-adjusted p = 0.74). The **relative** claim stands separately: vs the adopted rs_top2 rule it is a clear PASS — the production rule is provably backwards (selection edge +0.651R vs −0.418R) — but it inherits the same multiplicity caveat, and ranking never binds live, so there is no decision to make yet.

## What this changes from the validation handover

1. **The +0.239R baseline is overstated ~2× by universe selection.** Every gate calibrated to it is miscalibrated. Selection-neutral is roughly +0.05–0.12R.
2. **The research numbers are not a prior for the forward test.** The backtests don't implement Mode B exits at all; the forward test is the first real measurement of the frozen system — treat it as starting from ~zero evidence, not from +0.239R.
3. **Lone-wolf's published numbers used a leaky RS convention.** Strict point-in-time edge is ~14% smaller, and the "blocked trades are dead money (−0.0183R)" line should not stand (strict PIT: +0.0616R). The live overlay already uses the strict convention, so live results will track the weaker numbers.
4. **Nothing clears a multiple-testing correction.** All three candidates are ~1.5σ effects; the honest posture is MAYBE-pending-live-data across the board, not PASS.
5. **Profit is 10 trades in 3 months.** The handover didn't convey this concentration: without 2025 the baseline is 0.085R; 2026 ran −0.242R.

## The single most important fix

**Re-baseline on a point-in-time universe before the ~50-signal checkpoint.** It is the largest single distortion (~half the baseline), it miscalibrates every expectancy gate, and without it the checkpoint verdict will be judged against an inflated number. Concrete method (research-side only, frozen V5.4 untouched): fix the universe by rule on 2023-09-30 (e.g. Nasdaq-100 constituents or top-300 by dollar volume as of that date, documented in `research_notes/UNIVERSE.md`); keep delisted names through their last trading day via a survivorship-inclusive source; new listings enter only from listing date with ≥60 4H bars; maintain a corporate-action table for ticker changes/reuse; keep Mike's 14 as a reporting overlay only — never the validation universe alone. Secondary fixes, in order: re-run P10/lone-wolf under strict PIT and amend the README; fix Mode B's gap-through-stop fill (currently fills at the stop price — optimistic); re-run headline studies under the actual Mode B exit stack; move to per-trade 2-leg costs; add an explicit same-bar tie-break rule to production docs.
