# Next research idea — 2026-09-25

## The idea: stale-FVG invalidation (fresh gaps only)

**What it is, in one paragraph:** The 4H FVG-as-entry variant — the only entry
toggle that ever added edge (+0.285R vs +0.239R baseline, now running as the
live paper sidecar) — fires whenever price sits in a bull fair-value gap,
no matter how old that gap is. The Pine port keeps `lastBullFvgLow/High`
persisting until the *next* bull FVG forms, so a gap printed weeks ago counts
the same as one printed yesterday. This test asks: does the FVG edge live only
in *fresh* gaps? Variant: take FVG-entry signals only when the gap formed
within the last 10 bars (≈5 trading days on the 2-bars/day 4H convention);
ignore signals leaning on older, stale gaps.

**Why it might work (the mechanism, not vibes):** A fair value gap is an
unfilled imbalance — the edge thesis is that price revisits it *soon*, before
the market accepts the new level. Every bar a gap sits unfilled without being
revisited is evidence the market has accepted the price and the imbalance no
longer matters. That is edge decay, the same phenomenon behind the parked
signal-decay diagnostic: predictive power should be highest right after
formation and fade with age. The system already checks the gap is *nearby in
price* (the buy-zone widening requires |close − fvgHigh| ≤ 3·ATR); this checks
it is *fresh in time*. If stale gaps are noise, cutting them should raise the
sidecar's expectancy without losing many trades.

**Exactly how to test it:**
- Universe: the same 14-stock watchlist, 4H session bars (09:30/13:30 ET),
  Oct 25 2023 → Sep 23 2026, same 1H-derived cache as the entry-timing study.
- Engine: `pine_backtest.py` imported UNMODIFIED. Copy the FVG port from
  `pine_entry_timing_backtest/run_entry_timing.py` into the new study folder
  and add one field: the bar index where the current `lastBullFvg` formed
  (FVG age = signal bar − formation bar). Research-port change only; nothing
  frozen is touched.
- Variants (all with identical Mode B exits, next-bar-open fills, 4bps +
  25bps costs, $10k / 1%-per-trade / max-5 / 5%-risk portfolio sim):
  - BASE: all-off canonical Hybrid, rerun in-study (never reuse old numbers).
  - FVG-ALL: current sidecar behavior, rerun (expect ≈266 trades, ≈+0.285R).
  - FVG-FRESH: FVG-entry signals only when FVG age ≤ 10 bars. Pre-registered
    cutoff — no tuning across K. (Ages 5 and 20 may be reported as secondary
    context only, not decision criteria.)
- Attribution: split FVG-entry trades into fresh (≤10) vs stale (>10) buckets;
  report n, win%, expectancy per bucket, and what share of the 29 marginal
  FVG trades each bucket holds.
- **Success gate:** FVG-FRESH beats FVG-ALL by >0.02R at 25bps *and* clears the
  +0.15R deployment gate. A null or negative result is a clean kill — it means
  the sidecar needs no age filter.

**What could go wrong (honest):**
- Thin sample: only 29 of the 266 FVG-combo trades are marginal (non-canonical).
  If the edge lives in the marginals, the fresh/stale split may be ~15 vs ~14
  trades — noise dominates and the honest verdict is "can't tell," not a win.
  Mitigation: also bucket *all* 266 FVG-entry trades by age and report n
  prominently; do not claim victory on tiny buckets.
- Multiple comparisons: trying several age cutoffs until one wins is
  data mining. Hence one pre-registered K=10.
- The edge may sit in canonical+FVG overlap trades rather than marginals —
  then age-filtering changes little and attribution will show it plainly.
- In-sample on the 14 development names: even a win only refines the sidecar
  *hypothesis*. Adoption would still need the live paper sidecar to confirm
  out-of-sample. This is lab work, never a system change.

## Why this idea and not the others

- **Grade-based filtering (do B-grades underperform A?)** is the highest-stakes
  question in the program — but it already belongs to the forward test by
  design (C-grade decision gate at ~50–100 signals, market-gate Stage 2).
  Backtesting grades on the 14-name development set risks circularity; the
  out-of-sample answer is coming on schedule. Not the lab's job.
- **ATR-regime filter** (skip signals when ATR is historically extreme): R is
  scale-free and stops are ATR-scaled, so the mechanism has to be purely
  behavioral ("buying volatility spikes is buying exhaustion") — plausible but
  much weaker than edge decay, and it risks cutting the best trends.
- **Session effect** (09:30-bar vs 13:30-bar signals): real mechanism
  (gap-driven breakouts fade), but ~118 trades per bucket is thin and it
  smells of data mining; a filter that discards half the signals needs a huge
  effect to earn its keep.
- **VolumeOK/safeToEnter toggles off**: legitimate question, but those are
  Mike's chosen live input settings — lower priority than improving the
  system, and politically noisier.
- Exits, shorts, hedges: closed by Mike's explicit lines. TP1/Profit Protect
  tuning: closed as overfit risk.

Stale-FVG wins because it is the *flagged-but-untested* item on the candidate
board, it refines the *only* variant that ever added entry edge, it has a real
decay mechanism (not vibes), it is cheap to run on existing cache, and its
success gate is pre-registered and falsifiable. One clean question, one clean
answer.
