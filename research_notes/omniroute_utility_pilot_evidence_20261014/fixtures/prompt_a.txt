We study short-horizon return continuation in large-cap U.S. equities. Using 4-hour bars for the 214 current constituents of a large-cap index from January 2015 to December 2024, we form portfolios sorted on the prior 4-hour return. The top-minus-bottom quintile spread earns an average next-4-hour return of 11.2 basis points (t-statistic 3.4). The effect is concentrated in the first hour after portfolio formation and is absent in a matched small-cap sample. A zero-cost implementation earns a monthly Sharpe ratio of 0.8 before transaction costs.
