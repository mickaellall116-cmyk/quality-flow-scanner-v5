## The blocker — exact statement and root cause

**Blocker:** the C0 fidelity invariant — the independent-control cross-check asserted by the frozen stack (`pine_stack.main` aborts on it) and used as a preflight gate in -08 — **fails on the real 43-name candidate set under this study's preregistered candidate order** (pilot-first + child-manifest order): Δ final equity −51.65, cap skips 343 vs 323.

**Root cause (proven by diagnostic, `diag_fidelity.py`):** the two frozen simulators process coincident-timestamp entries in different orders. `pine_backtest.simulate_portfolio` (the control) iterates entries in **candidate-list order**; the stack's C0 path orders them by the **canonical key (symbol, signal_idx)**. The corrected stack C0 is itself input-order invariant (final equity 11,803.6785 under all three orders tested). The control is not: final equity 11,752.03 (study order), 10,529.48 (UNIVERSE_X order), 11,803.68 (alphabetical order). Under a candidate list in **canonical (symbol, signal_idx) order the invariant passes EXACTLY** — Δ = 0.0000, cap skips 323 = 323, risk skips 72 = 72. The divergence is therefore not a fork defect and not a data defect: it is an **order-conditional cross-validation instrument** meeting a candidate order the frozen configuration never pinned. In every prior fidelity check the caps either never bound (-08: 61/61 taken) or the sample was too small to expose the order sensitivity; at 586 candidates with binding caps it is decisive.

**What is NOT blocked:** the ranked C1 simulator — the study configuration proper, on the -09 fork — was proven **input-order invariant on the real 586 candidates**: identical taken set (sha256 of the taken (symbol, signal_idx) set identical) and identical rank/cap/risk/sector skip counts under the study order, a seeded shuffle, and canonical order. The portfolio layer binds hard on this universe (hundreds of rank skips; sector and risk gates both fire). So the corrected engine is well-defined; what cannot be honestly claimed today is the *independent cross-validation* of its accounting under the preregistered order.

**Unblocking condition (one pin, adjudication's to make):** pin the study's candidate-list order as **canonical (symbol, signal_idx)** — equivalently candidate generation in alphabetical symbol order — as part of the frozen configuration (GO gate 4 expressly freezes "same-time allocation order"). Under that single pin the fidelity evidence above already passes exactly, the ranked sim is order-invariant, and every other gate in this package stands PASS and reusable; a successor task re-verifies P0–P4 and executes the single run. This task did not adopt the pin itself: changing the candidate order after watching the gate fail would alter the frozen configuration mid-flight — precisely what the fail-closed discipline exists to prevent.
